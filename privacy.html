ToughLove: Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 1, 2026

Welcome to the ToughLove app for Android!

ToughLove is a screen time reduction app that helps you build healthier phone habits through personalized blocking schedules, motivational audio nudges, and a 6-week goal-based reduction plan.

As someone who values digital wellbeing, I take your privacy seriously. I know how frustrating it is when apps collect your data without your knowledge.

Data collected by the app

I hereby state, to the best of my knowledge and belief, that I have not programmed this app to collect any personally identifiable information. All data created by you (the user) — including your blocked apps list, schedules, screen time goals, motivation preferences, and daily usage tracking — is stored locally on your device only, and can be simply erased by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it. No analytics software is present in the app. No data is transmitted to any server, third party, or external service.

Explanation of permissions requested in the app

Display Over Other Apps

Required to display the blocking overlay on top of blocked apps when you open them during a scheduled time or after your daily quota is exhausted. This overlay presents you with options to close the app or pick a time-limited window. Without this permission, the app cannot intervene when you open a blocked app. Has to be granted by the user manually; can be revoked at any time from Settings.

Usage Access

Required to read your screen time data during onboarding. This is used solely to calculate your current daily average across the apps you select, which becomes the starting point for your 6-week reduction plan. The app reads aggregate usage data for the past 30 days; it does not access any content within those apps. Has to be granted by the user manually; can be revoked at any time from Settings.

Accessibility Service

This is the core permission that enables ToughLove to detect which app is currently in the foreground. When you open a blocked app during a scheduled time, the accessibility service triggers the blocking overlay. The service does NOT read screen content, keystrokes, passwords, messages, or any personal data. It only observes which app window is in the foreground. Can be revoked at any time from Settings.

Notifications

Required to send you local notifications when you've used 50% or 90% of your daily screen time quota. These notifications are generated entirely on-device. No notification data is sent externally. Has to be granted by the user manually on Android 13 and above; can be revoked at any time.

Foreground Service

Enables the app to run a background service that monitors app usage throughout the day. This is necessary to track your daily screen time against your quota and to trigger blocking when needed. Permission automatically granted by the system; cannot be revoked by the user.

Query Installed Apps

Required to list installed apps on your device during onboarding so you can select which apps to block. The app does not transmit this list anywhere.

Data stored on your device

  • Blocked apps list — The apps you selected during onboarding to be blocked.
  • Schedules — Time ranges you configured for active blocking.
  • Daily usage — Seconds spent on blocked apps each day, used to track quota.
  • Screen time goal — Your current average and 6-week reduction target.
  • Motivation style — Your preferred audio nudge style.
  • Preferences — Onboarding completion status, cooldown timestamps, and other settings.

All of the above is stored locally on your device and is never transmitted off your device.

Third-party sharing

This app does not share, sell, or transmit any user data to any third party. There are no analytics SDKs, advertising networks, or tracking tools embedded in the app.

Children's privacy

ToughLove is intended for users aged 18 and above. The app does not knowingly collect any data from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected with a new effective date at the top of this page.

If you find any security vulnerability that has been inadvertently caused by me, or have any question regarding how the app protects your privacy, please send me an email, and I will surely try to fix it/help you.
